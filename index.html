How We Check Facts

Four stages, one destination: the truth.

01

Find

We spot viral claims, trending stories and suspicious information circulating online and offline.

02

Investigate

We trace the claim to its origin, examine the available evidence and dig into the surrounding context.

03

Verify

We cross-check what we find against reliable, independent sources and expert input.

04

Share

We publish clear, accessible findings so people can understand — and share — the truth.
